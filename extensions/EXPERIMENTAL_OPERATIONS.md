# Experimental Operations Beyond the ADGL 0.3.0 Core Profile

These are preserved from the broader design discussion but are **not all normative in the current paper**.

## Admission / targeting
INCLUDE_ONLY, CONDITIONAL_ALLOW, EXCLUDE_AUTHOR

## Transformation / privacy
REDACT, MASK, PSEUDONYMIZE, ANONYMIZE, FILTER_FIELDS, TRANSFORM, NORMALIZE, SANITIZE

## Influence
PREFER, BOOST, DOWNRANK, WEIGHT, CAP_INFLUENCE, CONTEXTUALIZE, CHALLENGE

## Sampling / evaluation
SAMPLE_RANDOM, SAMPLE_SYSTEMATIC, SAMPLE_STRATIFIED, SAMPLE_CLUSTERED, SAMPLE_WEIGHTED, SAMPLE_TEMPORAL, SAMPLE_BY_RISK, HOLDOUT, SPLIT, BENCHMARK_AGAINST, BLIND, DOUBLE_REVIEW

## Distribution
BALANCE, REWEIGHT, CAP_CATEGORY, MINIMUM_QUOTA, DEDUPLICATE, COLLAPSE_CLUSTER

## Time
WINDOW, SNAPSHOT, USE_AS_OF, DECAY_WEIGHT, FREEZE

## Provenance / identity
VERIFY_IDENTITY, REQUIRE_CHAIN_OF_CUSTODY, TRUST_DOMAIN, FLAG_ORIGIN, REVOKE_SOURCE

## Contamination
SCAN_CONTAMINATION, FLAG, QUARANTINE_IF, DECONTAMINATE, TRACE_DERIVATION, DETECT_LEAKAGE, INVALIDATE_DERIVATIVES, REPROCESS

## Analysis
COUNT_DISTINCT, SEGMENT, JOIN_CONTEXT, CALCULATE, TEST, ESTIMATE, ANNOTATE

## Human governance
REQUEST_EVIDENCE, OVERRIDE_DECISION, ANNOTATE_DECISION, APPEAL, REVOKE_APPROVAL

## Additional control
MATCH, SWITCH, BEFORE, AFTER, PIPE, PARALLEL, WAIT_FOR_ALL, WAIT_FOR_ANY, FOR_EACH_MATCH, WHILE, REQUIRE_ALL, REQUIRE_ANY, DEPENDS_ON, BLOCK_UNTIL, ON_FAILURE, RETRY, CONTINUE, ABORT, SKIP

An extension should become normative only after its semantics, precedence, failure behavior and conformance fixtures are defined.
