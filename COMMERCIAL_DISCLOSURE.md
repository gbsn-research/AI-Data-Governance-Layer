# Commercial interest and implementation disclosure

GBSN Research designs and maintains the AI Data Governance Layer (ADGL) as an openly published research specification intended for broad implementation. GBSN Research also develops KEE (Knowledge Execution Engine), a separate proprietary commercial runtime that implements and extends parts of ADGL. GBSN Research therefore has a direct commercial and reputational interest in ADGL adoption and perceived value.

The public ADGL specification, cases, conformance suite, Reference Toolkit, and associated evaluation were produced by GBSN Research. KEE was also produced by GBSN Research. Results from either implementation are producer-authored evidence and are not independent certification or third-party validation.

KEE development exposed execution-integrity and trust-boundary issues that informed candidate checks C26-C36. KEE-specific behavior is not normative ADGL behavior unless accepted into a future public specification. KEE is not required to implement ADGL or to run the public conformance fixtures. The proprietary product must not be treated as the normative definition of conformance.
